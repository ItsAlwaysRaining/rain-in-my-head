# Experiment Design: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 8 · Detailed designs for the top 3 experiments.

## Experiment 1: Problem interviews
- **Who:** 5 Spotify users with 5+ years of history. Bonus: 2 who use Last.fm.
- **Recruit message:** "I'm doing a UX case study on music playlists. Could I ask you 25 minutes of questions about how you find music? No selling, just listening."
- **Questions:**
  1. Tell me about the last time a Spotify playlist felt wrong.
  2. When did you last hear a song you'd forgotten you loved? How did it happen?
  3. How do you find old favorites now?
  4. Have you ever wondered why Spotify picked a song? What did you do?
  5. Have you ever built a playlist by hand to fix this? Walk me through it.
- **Record:** problem quotes, workarounds, and the exact words they use.

## Experiment 2: Paper "why" test
- **Setup:** pick 10 tracks from your own history. For each, write (a) your reason ("140 plays in spring 2014, none since 2017") and (b) a Spotify-style note ("Because you like indie folk").
- **Run:** show the pairs in random order. Ask "Which makes you want to press play?" and "Which feels more like it knows you?" (rate 1–5).
- **Pass:** at least 4 of 5 testers prefer (a) on most pairs.

## Experiment 3: Concierge playlist
- **Setup:** ask 5 friends for their Last.fm username. Run the forgotten favorites query by hand and make each of them a Spotify playlist with reasons in the description.
- **Follow up on day 7:** "Did you play it again? Which songs? Would you want another one, and for what era?"
- **Pass:** at least 3 of 5 replayed it and asked for another.
