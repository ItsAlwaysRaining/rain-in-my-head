# Kill Criteria: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 8 · "Kill" here means *pivot the case study's angle*, not stop the project.

1. **3 or fewer of 5 interviewees** confirm the loop or forgotten-favorites pain → pivot to the "time-machine as a personal music memoir" angle.
2. **2 or fewer of 5** prefer your reasons over generic notes (Experiment 2) → drop "why" as the headline and lead with rediscovery.
3. **2 or fewer of 5** finish setup (Experiment 4) → the target becomes Self-Hosters only, and the case study is about designing for that limit.
4. **Spotify blocks playlist writes** in development mode → switch to M3U/JSPF export plus ListenBrainz.
5. **Under 20 GitHub stars** two weeks after launch → fine for a portfolio; stop marketing and focus on the case study.
6. **No shipped MVP after 6 weeks** → cut the time-machine and rule builder from scope.
