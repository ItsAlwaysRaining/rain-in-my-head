# Validation Playbook: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 8 · Experiments are ordered cheapest first. Experiment #1 is the deferred interviews.

| # | Experiment | Assumption tested | Method | Metric | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Problem interviews** (deferred from Phase 3.7) | People feel stuck in a loop and miss old favorites | 5 calls of about 25 minutes each with Spotify users who have 5+ years of history | Problem confirmed without prompting; workarounds people already use | 4 of 5 confirm, 2+ have workarounds | 3 or fewer confirm |
| 2 | **Paper "why" test** | Specific reasons beat Spotify's generic notes | Show 10 tracks with your reasons next to the same tracks with Spotify-style notes, in random order | Preference and "feels like mine" (1–5) | 4 of 5 prefer yours | 2 or fewer prefer yours |
| 3 | **Concierge playlist** | People will listen to and keep a forgotten favorites playlist | You build playlists for 5 friends from their Last.fm history | Kept and replayed after 7 days | 3 of 5 replay it | 1 or fewer |
| 4 | **Setup usability test** | Non-technical users can finish setup | A clickable prototype of the guided setup, think-aloud, 5 users | Completion, time, errors | 4 of 5 finish in under 20 minutes | 2 or fewer finish |
| 5 | **Launch post** | Self-hosters care | An r/selfhosted "Introducing" post with a GIF | Upvotes, GitHub stars, forks in 2 weeks | 100+ stars | Under 20 stars |
