## One sentence
**Rain in My Head turns your whole listening history into Spotify playlists that tell you why every song is there.**

## Customer profile (Long-Time Listener)
- **Jobs:** rediscover music they loved; revisit an era of their life; trust what they're listening to.
- **Pains:** stuck in a loop; forgotten favorites; AI songs; no "why."
- **Gains:** "these are *my* songs"; nostalgia; feeling in control; something worth sharing.

## Value map
| Pain or gain | Reliever or creator | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Stuck in a loop | Picks from 10+ years, not recent weeks | Rediscovery playlists |
| Forgotten favorites | Finds songs that were loved and then dropped | "Forgotten favorites" rule |
| No "why" | A specific reason on every track | Why-this-song |
| AI songs | Only songs you've actually played | History-only source |
| Nostalgia | Explore and replay any era | Time-machine |
| Control | Rules you can see and adjust | Rule builder |
