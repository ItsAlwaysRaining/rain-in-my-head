# Positioning: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 4 · **Framework:** April Dunford

1. **Competitive alternatives:** Spotify's algorithmic playlists (Prompted Playlist, Discover Weekly, DJ); ListenBrainz Weekly Jams; making playlists by hand from stats.fm or Last.fm.
2. **Unique attributes:** reasons drawn from your own history on every track; eras you can explore; rules you can see; history from many services through Last.fm; data you own in SQLite; free and open source.
3. **Value:** playlists that sound like *your* life, not the last month, and that you can trust because you can see why each song is there.
4. **Best-fit customers:** analysts who want control of their own data: comfortable in a terminal, familiar with git and open-source projects, with 5+ years of Last.fm history. Secondary: the long-time listeners they run it for. (Updated 2026-11-18.)
5. **Market category:** **"Personal playlist engine."** It's not a stats app and not an AI DJ.

## Positioning statement
For analysts who want control of their own listening data, Rain in My Head is an open-source, local playlist engine that loads your whole history into a database you can query and builds Spotify playlists that say why every song is there. Unlike Spotify's AI playlists, it runs on your machine, uses only music you've actually loved, and shows its logic.

## Onliness test
Rain in My Head is the **only** open playlist engine that **explains every pick using your own decades of listening history, stored in a database you own** for **analysts who want control of their data**.
