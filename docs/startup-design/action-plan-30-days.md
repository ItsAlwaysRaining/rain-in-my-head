# 30-Day Action Plan: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

## Week 1: Customer discovery
1. Send the recruit message from [experiment-design.md](06-validation/experiment-design.md) to 10 friends or contacts with 5+ years of Spotify use.
2. Post once in r/lastfm asking for 2 or 3 people to do a 25-minute chat.
3. Run 5 interviews and save each one as `00-intake/interviews/interview-N.md`.
4. Write `00-intake/interview-synthesis.md` and apply the interview gate (pass if 4 of 5 confirm the problem).

## Week 2: Validation experiments
1. Experiment 2: write 10 pairs of reasons (yours against generic) from your own history, and test them with 5 people.
2. Experiment 3: build 5 forgotten favorites playlists by hand for friends.
3. Technical spike: create one playlist through `POST /me/playlists` and `/items` from your dev app.
4. Request your Spotify extended streaming history export (it can take up to 30 days).

## Week 3: MVP scoping
1. On day 7, follow up with the 5 concierge playlist friends.
2. Prototype the guided setup in Figma (Last.fm first, then the forgotten favorites preview, then Spotify).
3. Experiment 4: think-aloud test of the setup prototype with 5 users.
4. Update [feature-prioritization.md](04-product/feature-prioritization.md) with what you learned.

## Week 4: Decision and next phase
1. Check results against [kill-criteria.md](06-validation/kill-criteria.md).
2. Decide the headline: "why this song" or rediscovery.
3. Start building in the order from the prioritization doc.
4. Draft the case-study outline: problem, research, constraint (setup), design, results.
