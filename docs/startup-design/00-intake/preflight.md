# Pre-Flight Check: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 0.5

- ⚠️ Established competitors: stats.fm (4.6★ from 44.5k App Store ratings), Last.fm, ListenBrainz, Raygum, Free Your Music Stats, and self-hosted YourSpotify, Maloja, and Koito.
- ⚠️ Spotify API: since 15 May 2025, extended access requires a registered business and at least 250k monthly active users. Development mode is capped at 5 to 25 users, and it got tighter again in February 2026. Use Last.fm or Spotify export files instead.
- ⚠️ Past failures were B2B music analytics: Next Big Sound shut down in 2021 and Asaii was acquired by Apple in 2018. These don't apply directly to a consumer tool.
- ✅ No legal blocker for analyzing your own data. Spotify's Developer Terms v10 ban training AI models on Spotify content.
