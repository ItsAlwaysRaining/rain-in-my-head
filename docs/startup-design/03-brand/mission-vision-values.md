# Mission, Vision, and Values: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 5 · Built on [../02-strategy/positioning.md](../02-strategy/positioning.md)

**Chosen (2026-11-18):** Mission A and Vision A.

## Mission options
- **A (recommended):** Give people back the music they've loved, with the reason it's there.
- **B:** Make playlists that sound like your whole life, not just last month.
- **C:** Turn your listening history into music you can trust.

## Vision: pick one
- **A (recommended):** Everyone's listening history belongs to them and works for them.
- **B:** A world where no one gets stuck in an algorithm's loop.

## Values
1. **Show your work.** Every song has a reason you can read.
2. **Your history, your hands.** The data lives with you, in files you can open.
3. **Only what you've loved.** No filler, no promotions, no AI tracks.
4. **Easy to start, deep if you want.** Simple for listeners, with SQL for anyone who wants it.
