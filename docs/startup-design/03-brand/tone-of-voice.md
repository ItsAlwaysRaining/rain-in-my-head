# Tone of Voice: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 5

## Personality traits
Warm, specific, quietly nostalgic, honest, and unhurried.

## Voice principles
| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| **Specific:** "You played this 140 times in spring 2014." | Vague: "Based on your taste…" |
| **Warm:** "Remember this one?" | Cutesy: "OMG this bop!! 🎉" |
| **Plain:** "Connect Spotify (about 3 minutes)." | Technical by default: "Configure OAuth redirect URI." |
| **Honest:** "This needs Spotify Premium." | Hiding limits in small print |
| **Calm:** "Your history is ready." | Hype: "Unleash your music DNA!" |

## Writing samples
- **Playlist description (300 characters max):** "Winter 2016, rebuilt from 1,204 plays. Made by Rain in My Head. Every song here is one you loved."
- **A "why" note on a track:** "Top 10 for you in 2014 · last played March 2017."
- **Empty state:** "Nothing here yet. Pick an era on the timeline to start."
- **Error:** "Spotify didn't accept the playlist (error 403). Check that your Spotify app has your account listed as a user."

## Vocabulary
| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| plays, listening history | scrobbles (use it only in technical docs) |
| era, season, year | time bucket |
| forgotten favorites | churned tracks |
| reason, why | algorithm, AI |
| rules | filters, query |
