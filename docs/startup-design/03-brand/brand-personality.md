# Brand Personality: Rain in My Head

Created: 2026-11-18
Last updated: 2026-11-18

**Phase:** 5

## Archetype
**The Sage with a touch of the Lover.** It's a thoughtful keeper of your memories. It explains instead of performing, and it cares about what music meant to you.

## Emotional attributes
Nostalgic, trustworthy, calm, intimate, and a little moody (like rain on a window).

## Visual direction (from the existing palette in [rain-in-my-head-palette.html](../../../rain-in-my-head-palette.html))
- **Night indigo** (`#2E3F7A`, dark background `#0C1221`) is the base: evening, headphones on.
- **Rain blue** (`#0878A6` / `#5CC8F0`) is for actions and "now" moments.
- **Lightning yellow** (`#B89B00`) is used sparingly, for highlights and storm mode.
- **Type:** Instrument Sans for the interface, IBM Plex Mono for numbers and the data views.
- **Motifs:** a timeline drawn like falling rain, with each play as a drop and each era as a storm.

## How it should feel compared with competitors
| Competitor | Their feel | Ours |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify | Loud, green, endless feed | Quiet, personal, finite |
| stats.fm | Scoreboard and leaderboard | A journal, not a scoreboard |
| ListenBrainz | Technical and dense | Calm, with depth on request |
| Wrapped | Once-a-year fireworks | Rain that's always there |
