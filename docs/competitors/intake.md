## Product
A free, open-source **personal playlist engine** that runs locally. It reads your whole Last.fm listening history, finds old obsessions, forgotten favorites, and past eras, and writes Spotify playlists that explain why every song is there. A hosted web app exists only as a UX portfolio piece.

## Market
Consumer music listening tools: stats apps, Spotify's algorithmic playlists, and open-source scrobbling and playlist tools. Global, English first.

## Pricing
Free, with no plans to charge. The user pays for Spotify Premium, which development mode requires.

## Differentiator
Specific "why this song" reasons from your own history, plus eras, rules you control, data you own, and Spotify output.
